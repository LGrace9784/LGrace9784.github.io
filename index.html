About Me

Hi, I’m Logan Grace, a dedicated software engineer with a passion for learning, problem-solving, and building innovative technology solutions. I enjoy continuously developing new skills and techniques to grow as a developer in an industry that is constantly evolving. I’m motivated by challenges and enjoy pushing myself outside of my comfort zone to continue improving both personally and professionally.

My background is a little different from the traditional software engineering path. I earned my undergraduate degree in Mechanical Engineering and am currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Software Engineering. After college, I spent much of my career working at a flight simulator company, primarily as a mechanical engineer. During that time, I became increasingly interested in the software side of development and discovered how much I enjoyed the problem-solving and creativity involved in building software systems. That experience ultimately motivated me to transition into software engineering and pursue it as a career.

Today, I spend much of my time outside of work completing my degree, building projects, and continuing to strengthen my technical skills, many of which are showcased throughout this website. I enjoy learning new technologies and finding opportunities to apply them in meaningful ways.

Outside of technology, I enjoy spending time outdoors with friends and family and exploring new places around the country. On any given summer day, you can probably find me outside playing golf. I also have a strong passion for learning beyond software engineering, whether that means picking up new skills, reading, or finding new ways to challenge myself.
